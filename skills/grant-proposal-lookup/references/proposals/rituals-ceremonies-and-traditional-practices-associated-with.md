6. What is the change you are trying to bring? What are the main challenges or problems you are trying to solve? Describe this change or challenges, as well as main approaches to achieve it. (required)

The project aims to safeguard the rich but underrepresented intangible cultural heritage of the Sri Vaidyanatha Parivara and Brahma Baidarkala deities of Mangaluru. Much of this traditional knowledge is preserved through oral transmission and community practices, making systematic digital documentation essential to prevent its gradual loss. Through field documentation, oral history collection, photographs, videos, and multilingual Wikimedia contributions, the project will make this heritage freely accessible to future generations, researchers, and the wider public. A community-centered approach, involving traditional families and ritual practitioners, will help ensure authenticity, cultural sensitivity, and the preservation of knowledge for generations to come.

7. What are the planned activities? (required) Please provide a list of main activities. You can also add a link to the public page for your project where details about your project can be found. Alternatively, you can upload a timeline document. When the activities include partnerships, include details about your partners and planned partnerships.

Planned activities are:

1. Community Consultation: Interact with traditional families, daiva paatries, ritual practitioners, and knowledgeable community members to gather information and obtain necessary permissions.

2. Field Documentation: Visit the sacred sites and document major rituals and ceremonies, including Dhwajarohana, Homa Seva, Balimurtha Utsava, various Nemotsavas, processions, and concluding ceremonies.

3. Audio-Visual Documentation: Capture photographs, videos, and audio recordings of ritual practices, traditional objects, ceremonies, and oral narratives, wherever permitted.

4. Oral History Collection: Record the historical background, cultural significance, customary practices, and traditional knowledge associated with the deities.

5. Content Preparation: Organize, verify, and prepare the collected information with the support of community members and subject experts.

6. Wikimedia Contributions: Upload photographs to Wikimedia Commons and create or improve relevant Wikipedia articles in English, Kannada, and Tulu.

7. Community Engagement and Outreach: Involve local youth, volunteers, and Wikimedia contributors in documenting and promoting this cultural heritage.

Planned Partnerships: The project will involvecollaboration with the concerned temple authorities, traditional families,daiva paatries, ritual performers, local cultural researchers, and Wikimediacommunity volunteers. These activities adopt a community-centered approach to ensure authenticdocumentation, respect for traditional practices, and wider digital access tothis valuable intangible cultural heritage.

17. What do you hope to learn from your work in this project or proposal? (required)

Through this project, we hope to learn how traditional knowledge associated with the Sri Vaidyanatha Parivara and Brahma Baidarkala deities can be effectively documented, preserved, and shared through Wikimedia platforms.

Our key learning questions are:

1. How can oral traditions, rituals, and customary practices be systematically documented while maintaining their cultural authenticity?

2. How can traditional families and community members actively participate in preserving their heritage?

3. What are the most effective ways to make this knowledge accessible through photographs, videos, and multilingual Wikimedia content?

4. How can digital documentation encourage younger generations to understand and appreciate their cultural heritage?

5. How can Wikimedia platforms improve the visibility and representation of lesser-documented local traditions?

We also hope to gain experience in community-based cultural documentation, oral history collection, and collaborative digital heritage preservation
