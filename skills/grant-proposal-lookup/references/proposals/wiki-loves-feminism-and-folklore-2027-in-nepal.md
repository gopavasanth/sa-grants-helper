6. What is the change you are trying to bring? What are the main challenges or problems you are trying to solve? Describe this change or challenges, as well as main approaches to achieve it. (required)

In Nepal, the Mithila region is home to diverse cultural traditions, including Sama Chakeva, Chhath, Jitiya, Mithila painting, traditional handicrafts, folk music, and community celebrations. Although these traditions are culturally significant, freely licensed photographs, multimedia resources, and well-referenced information covering many aspects of them remain limited.
We aim to improve this representation by encouraging communities to document their cultural heritage and contribute quality media to Wikimedia Commons.

- Gender and Knowledge Gaps

Women play an important role in preserving and transmitting Mithila's cultural heritage through folk arts, oral traditions, festivals, music, and traditional craftsmanship. Despite these contributions, women's experiences, artistic achievements, and participation in cultural traditions are not always adequately documented on Wikimedia platforms. Through Feminism and Folklore activities, we seek to increase coverage of notable women, women-led cultural practices, and their contributions to Mithila's heritage, while encouraging more women to participate as Wikimedia editors and photographers.

- Limited Participation in Local Communities

Many potential contributors in Maithili-speaking communities are unfamiliar with Wikimedia projects, free licenses, and the processes of contributing knowledge. The project will create accessible opportunities for new contributors through practical workshops, mentorship, and community-based activities conducted primarily in Maithili and Nepali.

- Expected Change

By the conclusion of the project, MWUG expects to achieve greater representation of Mithila's cultural heritage, improve content diversity on Wikimedia platforms, increase participation among women and new contributors, and strengthen the local Wikimedia volunteer network.

7. What are the planned activities? (required) Please provide a list of main activities. You can also add a link to the public page for your project where details about your project can be found. Alternatively, you can upload a timeline document. When the activities include partnerships, include details about your partners and planned partnerships.

* Activity 1: Wiki Loves Folklore 2027 – Maithili Community Campaign
MWUG will coordinate a local photography and media contribution campaign focused on the cultural heritage of Mithila.
Participants will be encouraged to document subjects including:
• Traditional festivals such as Sama Chakeva, Chhath, and Jitiya.
• Mithila painting and traditional handicrafts.
• Folk dances, traditional musical instruments, and performances.
• Traditional attire, jewelry, cuisine, and customs.
• Local artisans, cultural practitioners, and traditional occupations.
• Community celebrations and other forms of living cultural heritage.

The campaign will encourage participants to upload original, freely licensed, appropriately described, and accurately categorized media to Wikimedia Commons. Local recognition awards will be offered for outstanding contributions, with emphasis on originality, quality, educational value, and relevance to the project theme. The campaign will align with the international Wiki Loves Folklore rules and official competition period.

- Activity 2: Four Cultural Photo Walks

MWUG proposes to organize four community photo walks across selected heritage-rich locations in Madhesh Province.
Proposed locations:
• Janakpur, Dhanusha
• Rajbiraj and surrounding communities, Saptari
• Siraha district
• An additional culturally significant location in Madhesh Province, selected through consultation with local volunteers
Each photo walk will involve approximately 10–15 participants, with guidance from experienced Wikimedia contributors and photographers.
Participants will learn about ethical cultural documentation, image composition, metadata, free licensing, and appropriate categorization on Wikimedia Commons. The activities will prioritize traditional practices and subjects that are inadequately documented, rather than merely producing large numbers of photographs of already well-covered locations.

Activity 3: Feminism and Folklore 2027 – Maithili Wikipedia Writing Campaign
MWUG will organize a writing campaign on Maithili Wikipedia, aligned with the international Feminism and Folklore initiative. The campaign will focus on improving knowledge about folklore and the contributions of women to traditional culture. Priority topics will include notable women involved in folk arts, cultural performances, traditional crafts, literary traditions, and the preservation of regional heritage.
Participants will be guided in article creation, expansion, referencing, neutrality, and compliance with Wikipedia's notability and verifiability policies.
The campaign will use a dedicated coordination page and an appropriate Wikimedia contribution-tracking tool to record results. Local recognition will acknowledge contributors producing high-quality, properly referenced articles.

- Activity 4: Four Wikimedia Training Workshops and Edit-a-thons

MWUG will conduct four in-person training and contribution sessions:
Workshop 1 – Introduction to Wikimedia Commons
Training on account creation, uploading photographs, image descriptions, Creative Commons licensing, and Commons categories.
Workshop 2 – Folklore Photography and Documentation
Training on ethical photography, cultural documentation, media quality, and effective reuse of photographs in Wikipedia articles.
Workshop 3 – Feminism and Folklore Edit-a-thon
Practical training on Wikipedia editing, identifying reliable sources, expanding articles, and documenting women's contributions to Mithila culture.
Workshop 4 – Advanced Content Improvement and Mentorship
A session for improving article quality, adding references, integrating Commons media into Wikipedia, and developing contributors' long-term editing skills. Approximately 20–25 participants will be accommodated at each session, including new and experienced Wikimedians.

- Activity 5: Online Outreach and Mentorship

To reach contributors who cannot attend in-person activities, MWUG will conduct two virtual mentorship sessions. These sessions will support participants with account creation, media uploads, article improvement, licensing questions, and campaign participation. Communication channels, online community discussions, and social media will be used to provide guidance and publicize opportunities. Online collaboration with Maithili-speaking contributors in India will be encouraged without allocating grant funds for Indian travel or activities under this Nepal-based application.

- Activity 6: Local Awards and Recognition

The project will offer separate recognition programs for Wiki Loves Folklore and Feminism and Folklore. Recognition will consider contribution quality, relevance, licensing compliance, and community impact. Jury members will evaluate submissions using published criteria. Awards funded through this proposal will be distinguished from any prizes already provided by the international campaign to avoid duplication.

- Activity 7: Final Review and Community Learning

Following the competition period, MWUG will conduct a final review of contributions and organize a community learning and recognition session. The session will highlight selected photographs and articles, share outcomes, gather participant feedback, and discuss future initiatives. A public project report will summarize achievements, lessons learned, spending, and recommendations for future activities.

17. What do you hope to learn from your work in this project or proposal? (required)
