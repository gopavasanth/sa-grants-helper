6. What is the change you are trying to bring? What are the main challenges or problems you are trying to solve? Describe this change or challenges, as well as main approaches to achieve it. (required)

Through Wiki Loves Folklore Bangladesh 2027, we aim to preserve and promote Bangladesh’s diverse folk heritage by encouraging public participation in documenting cultural traditions and sharing photographs freely on Wikimedia Commons.

Bangladesh has a rich cultural heritage, but many regional festivals, traditional crafts, rituals, and indigenous customs remain underrepresented online. Limited digital documentation, unequal geographical coverage, and low public awareness of Wikimedia Commons are the main challenges we seek to address.

Our key objectives are to:

1. Increase freely licensed photographs of Bangladesh’s folk traditions and cultural practices on Wikimedia Commons.
2. Engage new contributors, particularly students, photographers, and cultural enthusiasts.
3. Improve the representation of lesser-known traditions and underrepresented regions.
4. Raise public awareness of open knowledge and the importance of cultural documentation.
5. Encourage quality contributions with accurate descriptions and appropriate categorization.

Our main approaches include organizing a nationwide photography competition, promoting it through social media, photography communities, educational institutions, and Wikimedia networks, and providing clear participation guidelines and online support for newcomers.
We have chosen these approaches because photography competitions offer an accessible and cost-effective way to engage people from diverse backgrounds. Building on previous Wikimedia photography initiatives, we aim to expand participation and encourage meaningful cultural contributions.

The expected long-term change is a more diverse and accessible digital collection of Bangladesh’s folk heritage, greater visibility for underrepresented cultural traditions, and stronger public engagement with Wikimedia projects. This will help preserve valuable cultural knowledge and make it available to future generations.

7. What are the planned activities? (required) Please provide a list of main activities. You can also add a link to the public page for your project where details about your project can be found. Alternatively, you can upload a timeline document. When the activities include partnerships, include details about your partners and planned partnerships.

The planned activities for Wiki Loves Folklore Bangladesh 2027 are:

1. Competition preparation (January 2027): Form the organizing team, finalize competition rules, prepare promotional materials, and coordinate with the international Wiki Loves Folklore campaign.

2. Outreach and partnerships (January–March): Collaborate with university photography clubs, professional and amateur photography communities, cultural organizations, and Wikimedia volunteers. We will approach potential partners to support promotion and encourage participation across Bangladesh.

3. Nationwide photography competition (February–March): Organize the main competition from 1 February to 31 March 2027, encouraging photographs of folk festivals, traditional crafts, rituals, indigenous cultures, and lesser-known regional traditions.

4. Digital promotion and participant support (February–March): Conduct social media campaigns, share participation guidelines, and provide online assistance through existing Wikimedia and photography community networks.

5. Submission review and judging (April): Verify submissions against competition rules and engage an independent jury to select winners based on photographic quality, cultural relevance, and documentation value.

6. Awards and recognition (April–May): Announce winners, distribute prizes, recognize contributors, and share selected photographs through community channels.

7. Reporting and follow-up (June): Evaluate participation, content contributions, geographical representation, and newcomer engagement. Encourage participants to continue contributing to Wikimedia Commons.

These activities build on previous Wiki Loves Folklore campaigns in Bangladesh, where photography competitions and community outreach successfully attracted contributors. Our approach prioritizes accessible participation, wider cultural representation, quality contributions, and long-term engagement .

9. Who are the target participants and from which community? How will you engage participants before and during the activities? How will you follow up with participants after the activities? (required)

The target participants of Wiki Loves Folklore Bangladesh 2027 are photographers, university students, cultural enthusiasts, Wikimedia contributors, and individuals interested in documenting Bangladesh’s folk heritage. We particularly aim to engage newcomers, young photographers, and participants from underrepresented regions and communities.

Before the competition, we will reach potential participants through social media campaigns, Wikimedia community networks, university photography clubs, and cultural organizations. We will share competition announcements, participation guidelines, and promotional materials to encourage wider involvement.

During the competition, we will maintain communication through social media and online channels, provide assistance with submission procedures, and share regular reminders and updates to encourage participation.

After the competition, we will announce the results, recognize contributors, and share selected photographs through community platforms. We will also encourage participants to continue contributing to Wikimedia Commons and engage in future Wikimedia initiatives.

Through these efforts, we aim to attract at least 50 participants, strengthen community engagement, and promote continued participation in documenting Bangladesh’s cultural heritage.

## Learning and metrics

Section link: https://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/Grants:Programs/Wikimedia_Community_Fund/Rapid_Fund/Wiki_Loves_Folklore_2027_in_Bangladesh_(ID:_24101144)#Learning_and_metrics

17. What do you hope to learn from your work in this project or proposal? (required)

Through Wiki Loves Folklore Bangladesh 2027, we aim to understand how a nationwide photography competition can improve cultural documentation, attract new contributors, and strengthen participation in Wikimedia Commons.
Our key learning questions are:

1. Community Engagement: How effectively can social media outreach, photography communities, and local partnerships attract new and existing Wikimedia contributors?

2. Cultural Representation: To what extent does the competition improve the documentation of lesser-known folk traditions and underrepresented regions of Bangladesh?

3. Content Quality: How well do participants follow Wikimedia Commons guidelines regarding image quality, licensing, descriptions, and categorization?

4. Contributor Retention: How many first-time participants continue contributing to Wikimedia projects after the competition?

5. Outreach Effectiveness: Which promotional channels and community partnerships generate the highest participation and quality contributions?

We will assess these questions through participation statistics, the number and quality of uploaded photographs, geographical coverage, newcomer contributions, and post-competition engagement.

These findings will help us identify effective outreach strategies, improve future competitions, and encourage sustainable participation in documenting Bangladesh’s cultural heritage.
