6. What is the change you are trying to bring? What are the main challenges or problems you are trying to solve? Describe this change or challenges, as well as main approaches to achieve it. (required)

Prayagraj hosted [[1] (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2025_Prayag_Maha_Kumbh_Mela) Maha Kumbh in 2025], one of the largest religious and cultural gatherings in the world. The event received extensive photographic coverage, with thousands of photographs shared across news websites, social media platforms, personal blogs, and other online channels. However, much of this visual documentation remains under restrictive copyright or without an explicit free licence. While these photographs are visible online, they cannot automatically be reused, adapted, or incorporated into Wikimedia projects and other free knowledge resources. This creates an important gap between the availability of photographs on the internet and the availability of freely licensed visual documentation that anyone can access, reuse, and share.

The experience of Maha Kumbh 2025 highlights the importance of documenting such significant cultural gatherings through open and freely licensed contributions. There is an opportunity to ensure that the cultural practices, traditions, people, architecture, and everyday experiences of Prayagraj are not only photographed but also preserved as part of the global knowledge commons. [[2] (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magh_Mela) Magh Mela] 2027 provides another opportunity to address this gap by engaging local photographers, students, and photography enthusiasts in creating and sharing freely licensed visual documentation through Wikimedia Commons. However, achieving this requires more than encouraging people to take photographs. Many potential contributors may be unfamiliar with open licences, Wikimedia Commons, copyright requirements, and the process of contributing to Wikimedia projects.

This proposal builds on our previous experience with both [[3] (https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=Campaign:wlc-26-pry) Wiki Loves Culture ] and Chai with Wiki. Our first local Wiki Loves Culture iteration in Prayagraj demonstrated the potential of community-led cultural documentation, resulting in 116 uploads to Wikimedia Commons. Similarly, Chai with Wiki has already been conducted through two editions, bringing together students and newcomers for introductory and practical learning sessions around open-source technologies and the Wikimedia ecosystem. These initial experiences provided an encouraging starting point and helped us understand the value of combining contribution opportunities with accessible, interactive learning spaces.

However, we see an opportunity to take these initiatives further by connecting them through a more structured programme. Rather than treating photography, Wikimedia training, and community gatherings as separate activities, we want to create a pathway through which a participant can move from being an interested photographer to becoming a confident Wikimedia contributor and, eventually, an active community member who can also support and engage others.

To achieve this change, we will adopt four connected approaches:

'1.'Encouraging community-led cultural documentation: Through the Wiki Loves Culture campaign during Magh Mela 2027, we will engage local photographers, students, and photography enthusiasts in documenting the cultural life of Prayagraj. An online orientation will introduce participants to Wikimedia Commons, free licences, copyright, photography guidelines, and the practical process of contributing. We will encourage documentation beyond commonly photographed landmarks, including everyday cultural practices, crafts, traditions, public spaces, and lesser-documented aspects of the gathering. This will help expand the availability of freely licensed visual material representing the region's cultural heritage.

2. Creating meaningful entry points for newcomers: We recognize that an interest in photography or open knowledge does not automatically translate into Wikimedia participation. People may need initial guidance, technical support, and a welcoming environment to make their first contributions. Through campaign support and the Chai with Wiki gathering, we will connect newcomers with experienced Wikimedians who can help them understand the Wikimedia ecosystem, overcome initial challenges, and explore different ways of contributing to projects such as Wikimedia Commons, Wikipedia, and Wikidata.

3. Strengthening skills, peer learning, and community leadership: Chai with Wiki will serve as a dedicated space for capacity building, mentoring, and experience sharing. We will invite experienced Wikimedians to conduct practical training sessions, guide newcomers, and share their experiences of contributing to and organizing activities within the Wikimedia movement. Alongside technical skills, we want participants to understand how community initiatives are planned, how volunteers are engaged, and how collaborative activities can be sustained. Through interactive discussions and mentoring, we hope to encourage interested participants to take initiative, organize future activities, and gradually develop community leadership skills.

4. Recognizing contributions and encouraging continued participation: An independent jury will evaluate the photographs submitted to the Wiki Loves Culture campaign against predefined criteria, and outstanding contributions will be recognized through awards during the offline Chai with Wiki gathering. Beyond recognizing photography skills, the gathering will provide an opportunity for participants to connect with one another, learn from experienced contributors, and discover further opportunities within the Wikimedia movement. Post-event mentoring and continued engagement will help interested participants build on what they have learned and remain connected with Wikimedia projects.

Ultimately...the change we hope to bring is not limited to increasing the number of photographs uploaded during Magh Mela. We want to strengthen local participation in documenting cultural heritage, make more visual knowledge freely accessible through Wikimedia Commons, and create opportunities for photography enthusiasts and newcomers to develop into confident contributors, collaborators, and future community organizers. By building on our previous experiences and connecting cultural documentation with learning, mentoring, and leadership development, we aim to contribute towards a more active and sustainable Wikimedia community in Prayagraj.

The project will combine a cultural photography campaign with community learning, capacity building, and leadership development. It will be implemented through a series of interconnected activities, beginning with campaign preparation and online orientation, followed by photography documentation during Magh Mela 2027, and concluding with an offline Chai with Wiki gathering in Prayagraj.

#### 1. Project Preparation and Community Outreach

We will begin by planning the Wiki Loves Culture campaign, preparing its guidelines, identifying documentation themes, and establishing the outreach strategy. We will connect with local photography enthusiasts, Wikimedia contributors, student communities, and other interested individuals in Prayagraj to encourage their participation.

We will also prepare the campaign infrastructure on Wikimedia Commons, including a dedicated campaign category, submission instructions, photography guidelines, and communication channels through which participants can receive assistance.

The outreach will focus on introducing people who are interested in photography and cultural documentation to the Wikimedia movement and creating an opportunity for them to contribute to freely accessible knowledge.

#### 2. Online Orientation and Introduction to Wiki Loves Culture

Section link: https://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/Community_Resources_and_Partnerships/India_Rapid_Project/Magh_Mela_2027_Documentation_and_Capacity_Building#2._Online_Orientation_and_Introduction_to_Wiki_Loves_Culture

Before the photography period begins, we will organize an online orientation session for interested participants. This session will introduce the Wiki Loves Culture campaign, explain its objectives, and demonstrate how photographs can contribute to documenting cultural heritage through Wikimedia Commons.

The session will cover:

- Introduction to Wikimedia Commons and the importance of free knowledge.
- Photography themes and guidelines for documenting Magh Mela 2027.
- Copyright, free licences, and image permissions.
- Practical demonstration of uploading photographs to Wikimedia Commons.
- Adding descriptions, categories, and relevant metadata.

Responsible and respectful photography, including privacy and applicable photography restrictions.

#### 3. Wiki Loves Culture: Magh Mela 2027 Photography Campaign

Section link: https://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/Community_Resources_and_Partnerships/India_Rapid_Project/Magh_Mela_2027_Documentation_and_Capacity_Building#3._Wiki_Loves_Culture:_Magh_Mela_2027_Photography_Campaign

Following the orientation, we will launch the Wiki Loves Culture photography campaign during Magh Mela 2027 in Prayagraj.

Participants will be encouraged to photograph and document different aspects of the gathering, including cultural practices, traditions, rituals, architecture, crafts, public spaces, and everyday experiences that reflect the cultural diversity and atmosphere of Magh Mela.

We particularly want to encourage documentation beyond commonly photographed landmarks and well-known activities, giving attention to lesser-documented cultural practices and everyday visual stories.

Participants will upload their photographs to Wikimedia Commons under the designated campaign category, using appropriate free licences and providing meaningful descriptions and relevant categories. The organizing team will provide guidance throughout the campaign to help participants with technical difficulties, uploads, licensing, categorization, and other contribution-related questions.

This activity will help make visual documentation of Magh Mela freely accessible and reusable while introducing new participants to practical contributions on Wikimedia Commons.

#### 4. Independent Jury Evaluation and Selection of Outstanding Contributions

Section link: https://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/Community_Resources_and_Partnerships/India_Rapid_Project/Magh_Mela_2027_Documentation_and_Capacity_Building#4._Independent_Jury_Evaluation_and_Selection_of_Outstanding_Contributions

After the photography campaign concludes, we will invite an independent jury comprising individuals with relevant experience in photography, cultural documentation, and/or Wikimedia projects to evaluate the submitted photographs.

The jury will assess eligible contributions against predefined criteria, including relevance to the campaign theme, photographic quality, originality, diversity of cultural documentation, and compliance with Wikimedia Commons requirements.

Based on the evaluation, outstanding photographs and contributors will be selected for awards and recognition. The selection process will be independent and transparent, with the evaluation criteria communicated to participants in advance.

The awards and recognition will be presented during the offline Chai with Wiki gathering. This will provide an opportunity to appreciate participants' efforts, celebrate their contributions, and motivate them to remain engaged with Wikimedia projects.

#### 5. Offline Chai with Wiki: Community Learning, Capacity Building and Leadership Development

Section link: https://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/Community_Resources_and_Partnerships/India_Rapid_Project/Magh_Mela_2027_Documentation_and_Capacity_Building#5._Offline_Chai_with_Wiki:_Community_Learning,_Capacity_Building_and_Leadership_Development

Following the photography campaign, we will organize an offline Chai with Wiki gathering in Prayagraj. Selected participants and outstanding contributors from the Wiki Loves Culture campaign will be invited to join experienced Wikimedians, newcomers, and other interested community members.

We envision Chai with Wiki as more than a concluding event or award ceremony. It will serve as a community learning and capacity-building space where participants can interact with experienced contributors, explore different Wikimedia projects, develop practical skills, and learn about opportunities to take up active roles in the Wikimedia movement.

The gathering will include the following components:

a. Newcomer Orientation and Guidance

We will provide dedicated guidance for newcomers who are interested in joining the Wikimedia movement but may not know where or how to begin. Experienced Wikimedians will introduce them to Wikimedia's values, community practices, contribution opportunities, and available support systems. Participants will receive guidance on creating accounts, making initial contributions, understanding community guidelines, and finding ways to stay involved.

b. Exploring the Wikimedia Ecosystem

We will organize introductory sessions showcasing different Wikimedia projects, including Wikipedia, Wikimedia Commons, Wikidata, and other relevant initiatives. Participants will learn about the purpose of these projects, how they complement one another, and the different ways they can contribute according to their interests and skills. This will help participants discover opportunities beyond photography and understand the wider free-knowledge ecosystem.

c. Practical Capacity-Building Training

We will invite experienced Wikimedians to serve as resource persons and trainers for hands-on learning sessions. These sessions will focus on practical contribution skills, such as improving photographs and metadata on Wikimedia Commons, categorization, adding images to relevant Wikipedia articles, contributing to Wikidata, understanding licensing, and following project-specific guidelines and policies.

The training will be designed to help participants apply what they learn through practical exercises and guided contributions rather than limiting the sessions to presentations.

d. Experience Sharing and Peer Learning

Experienced Wikimedians will be invited to share their journeys, experiences, challenges, lessons learned, and involvement in different Wikimedia projects and community initiatives. These interactions will give newcomers an opportunity to learn from real experiences, ask questions, and build connections with contributors who can support their future participation.

e. Community Leadership and Organizing Skills

An important focus of Chai with Wiki will be encouraging participants to move beyond individual contributions and explore community leadership opportunities. Through interactive discussions, experience sharing, and practical guidance from experienced organizers, participants will learn about planning Wikimedia activities, engaging volunteers, coordinating collaborative projects, facilitating learning sessions, and building sustainable community initiatives.

We aim to help interested participants develop the confidence, skills, and understanding required to take initiative, organize future activities, and gradually assume leadership responsibilities within their communities.

f. Awards and Recognition for Wiki Loves Culture Contributors

The gathering will also include an award and recognition segment for the outstanding photographs and contributors selected by the independent jury. Prizes and certificates will be presented to recognize their efforts in documenting Magh Mela 2027 and contributing visual knowledge to Wikimedia Commons.

This recognition will celebrate participants' achievements while also providing visibility to their work and encouraging others to participate in future cultural documentation initiatives.

By bringing together photography enthusiasts, newcomers, and experienced Wikimedians, Chai with Wiki will create an opportunity for cross-learning, mentorship, networking, and community building. We hope this gathering will help turn campaign participants into more confident and active contributors to Wikimedia projects.

#### 6. Post-Campaign Mentoring and Continued Engagement

Section link: https://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/Community_Resources_and_Partnerships/India_Rapid_Project/Magh_Mela_2027_Documentation_and_Capacity_Building#6._Post-Campaign_Mentoring_and_Continued_Engagement

Our engagement with participants will not end with the offline gathering. We will continue interacting with interested participants through online communication channels and follow-up mentoring. We will also collect participant feedback, review the campaign's outcomes, document the experiences and challenges, and assess the extent to which the activities helped participants develop new skills and remain engaged with Wikimedia projects.

Through this follow-up, we aim to create opportunities for continued participation and identify individuals interested in taking up future organizing, mentoring, and community leadership roles.
