6. What is the change you are trying to bring? What are the main challenges or problems you are trying to solve? Describe this change or challenges, as well as main approaches to achieve it. (required)

India's diverse cultural heritage includes the contributions of women to folklore, traditional knowledge, performing arts, oral histories, festivals, and community practices. However, these contributions remain underrepresented across several Indian-language Wikipedias, contributing to persistent gender and cultural knowledge gaps.

Through Feminism and Folklore India 2027, we aim to improve the representation of women and their contributions to India's living cultural heritage while strengthening participation in regional-language Wikimedia communities.

Key challenges include limited coverage of women in folklore, inadequate access to reliable regional-language sources, low participation among new and women contributors, limited technical training, and insufficient financial support for locally organised Wikimedia activities.To address these challenges, we propose a decentralised, community-led approach combining a national article-writing campaign with offline edit-a-thons and capacity-building workshops across nine language communities: Bengali, Tamil, Marathi, Telugu, Assamese, Tulu, Kannada, Odia, and Awadhi.

Our main approaches include:

1. National Article-Writing Campaign on Local Wikipedia: Encourage the creation and improvement of Wikipedia articles documenting women's contributions to folklore, traditional arts, cultural practices, and indigenous knowledge.
2. Regional Edit-a-thons: Locally coordinated edit-a-thons and workshops will be addressed to bridge gender and cultural content gaps across participating language Wikipedias in Bengali, Tamil, Marathi, Telugu, Assamese, Tulu, Kannada, Odia and Awadhi.
3. Capacity-Building Workshops: Provide practical training on Wikipedia editing, reliable sourcing, citations, notability, and content quality.
4. Community Partnerships: Collaborate with Wikimedia volunteers, educational institutions, women's groups, and cultural organisations to expand participation.
5. Recognition and Retention: Introduce local prizes, including quality article awards, alongside mentorship and follow-up activities to encourage sustained contributions.

Beyond increasing article numbers, the project aims to improve content quality, strengthen regional Wikimedia communities, increase women's participation, and establish sustainable networks of contributors documenting India's diverse cultural heritage.

The initiative aligns with the Wikimedia Movement Strategy 2030 by advancing knowledge equity, addressing systemic content gaps, and supporting inclusive participation across regional-language Wikimedia projects.

7. What are the planned activities? (required) Please provide a list of main activities. You can also add a link to the public page for your project where details about your project can be found. Alternatively, you can upload a timeline document. When the activities include partnerships, include details about your partners and planned partnerships.

Feminism and Folklore India 2027 will combine a national Wikipedia article-writing campaign with nine regional edit-a-thons, training workshops, community outreach, and recognition activities.

A. National Feminism and Folklore article-writing campaign

We will coordinate a nationwide writing campaign along with the nine organising language communities, encouraging contributors to create and improve articles about women in folklore, traditional knowledge, folk artists, cultural practitioners, rituals, festivals, and related subjects.Activities will include developing thematic article lists, preparing editing resources, coordinating regional organisers, providing guidance on reliable sources, and monitoring contributions.

Local prizes will recognise outstanding contributions, including both article quantity and quality, to encourage meaningful improvements to Wikipedia's cultural and gender representation.

B. Regional edit-a-thons and capacity-building workshops

We propose supporting local edit-a-thon and training workshops in these language communities. Each regional programme will include introductory Wikipedia editing sessions, hands-on article creation, reliable sourcing guidance, peer review, and discussions on gender representation.

This funding will support local event logistics, travel, venue arrangements, internet access, refreshments, and necessary training materials.

C. National outreach and contributor engagement

We will conduct targeted outreach through regional Wikimedia communities, universities, colleges, cultural organisations, women's networks, and social media.Campaign resources and communications will be made available in relevant regional languages wherever feasible.Special emphasis will be placed on engaging first-time editors, women contributors, students, and individuals from underrepresented communities.

D. Post-campaign follow-up and reporting

Regional coordinators will organise follow-up engagement sessions, encourage article improvements, and support contributors interested in continued participation.The national organising team will compile contribution statistics, participant feedback, activity reports, and financial documentation to evaluate the project's outcomes.

9. Who are the target participants and from which community? How will you engage participants before and during the activities? How will you follow up with participants after the activities? (required)

The project will primarily engage existing and new contributors from Indic communities. Some of the previous edition participant language communities were: Assamese Wikipedia, Awadhi Wikipedia, Bengali Wikipedia, Gujarati Wikipedia, Hindi Wikipedia, Fiji-Hindi Wikipedia, Kannada Wikipedia, Manipuri (Meitei) Wikipedia, Maithili Wikipedia, Marathi Wikipedia, Nepali Wikipedia, Odia Wikipedia, Santali Wikipedia, Sindhi Wikipedia, Tamil Wikipedia, Tulu Wikipedia, Telugu Wikipedia, Angika Wikipedia.

Target participants include women editors, students, educators, researchers, cultural enthusiasts, regional-language speakers, and volunteers interested in documenting women's contributions to folklore and cultural heritage.

We will prioritise engaging new contributors, women, and individuals from communities with limited access to Wikimedia training opportunities.Before the activities, regional organisers will conduct outreach through Wikimedia community channels, educational institutions, women's networks, and local cultural organisations. Participants will receive introductory resources, thematic article lists, and guidance on Wikipedia editing and reliable sourcing.

During the activities, participants will attend hands-on editing workshops and edit-a-thons, receiving support with article creation, citations, notability, neutral writing, and content improvement. Experienced editors will provide mentorship and peer review to help ensure contributions meet Wikipedia's quality standards.

After the activities, regional coordinators will maintain communication with participants through Wikimedia community channels, online follow-up sessions, and mentorship opportunities. Contributors will be encouraged to improve their articles, participate in future editing initiatives, and become active members of their language communities.

We will also collect participant feedback and monitor continued editing activity to understand contributor retention and identify opportunities for sustained engagement.

17. What do you hope to learn from your work in this project or proposal? (required)

Through Feminism and Folklore India 2027, we hope to understand how regional-language edit-a-thons, structured training, and community-led outreach can address gender and cultural knowledge gaps on Wikipedia while encouraging sustainable contributor participation.

Our key learning questions include:

- Participation: How effective are regional edit-a-thons and workshops in attracting new contributors, particularly women and underrepresented participants?Content Quality: How do structured editing workshops and mentorship improve the quality, reliability, and completeness of articles about women in folklore?
- Language Diversity: What challenges do smaller language communities face in documenting women's cultural contributions, particularly regarding reliable sources and technical support?
- Contributor Retention: What forms of mentorship, recognition, and follow-up engagement encourage participants to continue editing beyond the campaign?
- Community Partnerships: How can collaboration with educational institutions, cultural organisations, and local Wikimedia communities strengthen participation and content development?
- Recognition and Motivation: How effective are local prizes and quality article awards in encouraging meaningful contributions and sustained engagement?

We will collect quantitative and qualitative data through contribution tracking, participant registration, surveys, article quality reviews, and regional organiser reports.

The findings will help us improve future Feminism and Folklore campaigns, strengthen multilingual Wikimedia participation, and develop more effective approaches to addressing gender and cultural knowledge gaps across Indian-language Wikipedias.
